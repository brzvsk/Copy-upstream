Copy is free software under [GPL-3.0](LICENSE). Fork it, change it, sell it. That
is the point of the license and no part of this page takes it back.

The code is the grant. The name and the artwork are not. [NOTICE](NOTICE) states
that as an additional term under GPL-3.0 section 7(e), and this page explains what
it means without the legal register.

## What the marks are

- The name **Copy**, used for a clipboard manager or anything much like one
- The app icon, logo mark, wordmark, banner, favicon, and social preview image,
  including everything under `docs/assets/img/`
- The website at <https://tarikbc.github.io/Copy>
- The Homebrew cask name `tarikbc/tap/copy`

"Copy" is an ordinary word, and this policy claims nothing about the word itself.
It claims the name as it identifies *this* application, so that a person who
downloads something called Copy knows whose build they are running.

## You do not need to ask

- Say the name. Review it, blog about it, compare it to your own app, teach it,
  screenshot it, recommend it, or complain about it.
- Say your work derives from Copy. "Based on Copy", "a fork of Copy", and
  "originally by Tarik Caramanico" are all accurate and all welcome.
- Redistribute the official build unchanged, with its name and icon intact.
- Quote or reproduce [NOTICE](NOTICE). You are required to keep it anyway.
- Package the official build for a distro or a package manager, as long as it is
  the official build and you do not imply that I maintain your package.

## Rename before you ship

If you modify the code and distribute the result, ship it under your own name and
your own icon. Concretely, please do not:

- Publish a modified build called "Copy", or a name close enough to be mistaken
  for it
- Reuse the app icon, logo, or wordmark in your build or its listing
- Suggest that your build is the official one, endorsed by me, or supported by me
- List a modified build in an app store, a cask, or a directory under this name
- Register a domain, a social handle, or a package name that reads as the
  official channel for this app

## How to fork cleanly

1. Pick a new name and a new icon.
2. Replace the branding in `docs/assets/img/`, the app icon set, and the strings
   that carry the product name.
3. Point the website, the update feed, and the issue links at your own
   infrastructure. Do not ship a build that checks my Sparkle feed for updates.
4. Keep `LICENSE` and `NOTICE` as they are, and add your own copyright line and
   your modification date below mine in `NOTICE`. GPL-3.0 section 5(a) requires
   the modification statement; section 7(b) requires the attribution.
5. Tell people where the code came from. A link back costs you nothing and is
   the whole social contract of the GPL.

Do that and you have my goodwill, not just my license.

## Getting permission anyway

If you want to use a mark in a way this page does not allow, ask. Open an issue
or reach me through the profile on <https://github.com/tarikbc>. The answer to a
reasonable request is usually yes.

## Enforcement

I would rather send a message than a letter. If something here is being used in a
way that confuses users about who built or supports a build, I will ask for a
rename first and expect that to be the end of it.
